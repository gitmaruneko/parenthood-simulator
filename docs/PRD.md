# Product Requirements Document

## Product

Parenthood Simulator is a parenting life simulation game about everyday choices,
invisible labor, and family growth.

## Status

Initial project scaffold. Product requirements will be expanded as the game
mechanics and target experience are defined.

## Initial goals

- Model meaningful day-to-day parenting decisions.
- Make visible the trade-offs involving time, energy, relationships, and work.
- Support replayable family stories with different outcomes.
